# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads align the delivery team around technical direction, architectural decisions, and execution quality. They bridge product priorities with engineering implementation and help remove delivery bottlenecks.

### Responsibilities
- Own technical delivery planning and sequencing with PM and Product teams
- Guide architecture, design trade-offs, and technical standards
- Coordinate dependencies across engineering workstreams and integrations
- Support sprint execution, quality bar alignment, and risk mitigation
- Partner with developers on technical decisions, estimates, and delivery commitments

### Goals
- Deliver robust, maintainable solutions on schedule
- Improve engineering predictability and collaboration
- Maintain technical quality while supporting business priorities

### Typical Communication
- Engineering planning and design reviews
- Delivery health updates to PM and Product stakeholders
- Architecture and dependency discussions with cross-functional teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to translate roadmap priorities into feasible technical plans
- Supports Project Managers by clarifying dependencies, delivery risks, and milestone feasibility
- Collaborates with Developers to define implementation standards and technical trade-offs
- Coordinates with QA and Security partners to ensure readiness before release

---

## Security / Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads ensure that projects identify and mitigate risks related to data protection, security controls, policy requirements, and operational readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Review solutions for security, privacy, and compliance risks
- Define required controls, review checkpoints, and risk acceptance processes
- Partner with engineering and PMs on secure-by-design implementation
- Support incident response planning and remediation efforts
- Maintain awareness of relevant policies, legal requirements, and audit expectations

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk throughout the lifecycle
- Enable safe delivery without unnecessary delays
- Build confidence in the solution's operational integrity

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and architecture discussions
- Risk escalation updates with PM and leadership
- Compliance readiness checkpoints before release

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to ensure customer and regulatory requirements are reflected in scope decisions
- Partners with Engineering Leads to assess architecture, deployment, and data-handling risks
- Informs Project Managers of required controls, timing constraints, and escalation paths
- Collaborates with Developers on secure coding practices and validation steps

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the readiness, timing, and communication of deployments so teams can ship changes with fewer surprises and stronger operational awareness.

### Responsibilities
- Own release planning, sequencing, and execution windows
- Coordinate launch readiness checks across engineering, QA, operations, and stakeholders
- Draft release communication, rollback plans, and deployment notes
- Track post-release validation and issue triage after deployment
- Align releases with milestone and stakeholder expectations

### Goals
- Minimize release risk and production disruption
- Improve predictability and transparency for stakeholders
- Ensure smooth deployment and rollback readiness

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews
- Stakeholder announcements and deployment updates
- Post-release incident and monitoring coordination

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Aligns with Project Managers on release milestones, dependencies, and communications
- Works with Engineering Leads to validate technical readiness and rollback plans
- Coordinates with QA/Testing teams to confirm smoke tests and acceptance checks are complete
- Supports Product Managers and Stakeholders in communicating impact and timing

---

## Customer / Stakeholder Liaisons

### Role Summary
Customer and Stakeholder Liaisons ensure there is a consistent understanding of business goals, user impact, and project status across the people who influence or are affected by delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain stakeholder map, communication plan, and feedback loops
- Translate business needs, risks, and decisions into clear updates
- Surface user or customer concerns that may affect scope or priorities
- Coordinate decision-making and approval steps for milestones or changes
- Support adoption readiness and post-launch feedback collection

### Goals
- Keep stakeholders aligned on outcomes, trade-offs, and timing
- Improve confidence in delivery and operational readiness
- Ensure customer value remains central to project decisions

### Typical Communication
- Weekly or milestone-based status updates
- Stakeholder briefings and decision reviews
- Customer feedback synthesis and escalation discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides Product Managers with customer and stakeholder context to inform prioritization
- Works with Project Managers to manage communication cadence and escalation paths
- Keeps Engineering and Release teams aware of user impact and key dependencies
- Supports cross-functional alignment during approvals, demo reviews, and releases

---

## Platform / Operations Owners

### Role Summary
Platform and Operations Owners ensure that systems, environments, monitoring, and support readiness are in place for reliable project delivery and ongoing operational health.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain environment health, deployment tooling, and release capability
- Support monitoring, alerting, and observability for critical services
- Coordinate operational readiness, scaling, and incident response support
- Partner with engineering on infrastructure dependencies and risk mitigation
- Help establish runbooks and support handoff requirements for production changes

### Goals
- Improve service reliability and operational continuity
- Reduce friction between development and operations teams
- Support sustainable delivery with clear support ownership

### Typical Communication
- Platform health reviews and operational readiness checks
- Monitoring and incident response coordination
- Environment and deployment support updates with delivery teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Engineering Leads to support infrastructure decisions and deployment readiness
- Coordinates with Release Managers on deployment windows, monitoring, and rollback support
- Helps Project Managers understand operational risk and dependency management
- Supports Security and Compliance leads on environment controls and auditability

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles form a clearer accountability model for planning, execution, release, and stakeholder communication.
